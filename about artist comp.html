About Artist Compass

A beginner friendly guide that helps you find your way into any artist's music.

Why we made this

When you find an artist you like, it can be hard to know where to begin. Some have ten or more projects, and not every one is a good first listen.

Artist Compass points you to the right place. For each artist, we show their projects, name a good place to start, and list the songs worth hearing first.

How it works

1. Pick an artist

Choose one from the home page.

2. Start with the top songs

Each artist page ranks their biggest songs.

3. Explore the albums

Read the notable tracks and find what to hear next.
