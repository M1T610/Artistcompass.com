    Quick answers to common questions. Click a question to see the answer.

    What is Artist Compass?

    It's a beginner friendly guide to artist discographies. We list each artist's projects, point to a good place to start, and show the songs worth hearing first.

    Where should I start with an artist?

    Every artist page has a "How to start" section with our top picks. If you're not sure, begin with the top songs table, then try the album those songs come from.

    Are the song rankings official?

    No. The rankings and starting points are opinions, so they're a guide and not a rule. Trust your own ears.

    Which artists are on the site?

    Right now we cover The Weeknd, Future, J. Cole, Denzel Curry, Travis Scott, A$AP Rocky, Don Toliver and JID. More artists are listed on the Artist Coming Soon page.

    Can I listen to the music here?

    No. This site is a guide only, so you'll need to listen on a music app or website you already use.

    Who owns the images?

    All photos and album covers belong to their owners. They're used here only to help show each artist and project.

    Can I suggest an artist?
